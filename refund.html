Return & Refund Policy

We stand behind every InkVibe printer we build. This policy is designed to be fair, transparent, and business-friendly.

Last updated: March 1, 2026

InkVibe is committed to your complete satisfaction with every printer, supply, and service we provide. This Return and Refund Policy applies to products purchased directly from inkvibe.com or our authorized U.S. partners. Please read carefully.

To qualify for a return, your item must satisfy all of these conditions:

  • Returned within 30 calendar days from delivery date
  • In original, unopened packaging (unless the product is defective)
  • Unused, undamaged, and in fully resalable condition
  • Accompanied by original proof of purchase (order number or invoice)
  • Includes all accessories, cables, manuals, toner/ink starter kits, and original packaging

Printers that show signs of use beyond basic functionality testing (multiple print jobs, removed protective films, installed third-party supplies, etc.) may incur a restocking fee of up to 15% or be ineligible for return.

The following items are generally non-returnable unless received damaged or defective:

  • Ink cartridges, toner cartridges, maintenance kits and other consumables once opened
  • Custom-configured or special-order printers
  • Opened software licenses, firmware upgrades, or digital downloads
  • Items explicitly marked “Final Sale” or “Clearance”
  • Products damaged due to misuse, improper installation, unauthorized repair, or use of non-InkVibe supplies
  1. Contact our Returns team within 30 days at returns@inkvibe.com or 1-888-INK-VIBE. Include your order number and reason for return.
  2. We will issue a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number and detailed return instructions (usually within 1 business day).
  3. Pack the printer securely in its original packaging (or equivalent sturdy box), include all accessories, and clearly write the RMA number on the shipping label.
  4. Ship using a trackable carrier (we recommend insured shipping for high-value items).
  5. After inspection (usually 3–7 business days), we will process your refund or exchange.

Refunds are issued to the original form of payment:

  • Credit/Debit Cards: 5–10 business days after we receive and approve the return
  • PayPal / ACH / Wire: 3–7 business days
  • Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (wrong item shipped, defective on arrival)

We reserve the right to deny refunds or apply restocking fees if return conditions are not met.

For defective, damaged, or incorrect items, we will arrange a replacement at no extra charge (including prepaid return shipping).

For change-of-mind exchanges (different model/speed/capacity), standard return shipping fees apply and a restocking fee may be charged if the unit was powered on or supplies were installed.

  • Original shipping fee: Non-refundable unless we sent the wrong or defective product
  • Customer-paid return shipping: Required for change-of-mind returns
  • Defective / DOA / wrong item: We provide a prepaid return label or reimburse reasonable shipping costs
